Land Battle Chess

A classic two-player strategy board game reimagined with unique mechanics.

Welcome to the official game manual for Land Battle Chess. Whether you are a first-time player or looking to sharpen your strategy, this guide covers everything you need to get started and play confidently.

Land Battle Chess is a strategic board game in which all pieces start face-down. Players take turns flipping, moving, and capturing pieces based on military rank. Victory is achieved by capturing the opponent's Flag or eliminating all their movable pieces. Simple rules, deep strategy.
